Create a modern, high-fidelity homepage redesign concept for the SINAC (Sistema Nacional de Áreas de Conservación de Costa Rica) official website.

GOAL:
This is a UI/UX redesign proposal intended to present a modern vision for SINAC while preserving its institutional identity and public service functions.

The design should feel like a premium National Geographic, UNESCO World Heritage, and modern eco-tourism experience, but still maintain the credibility and trust expected from a government environmental institution.

DO NOT create a generic government portal.
DO NOT create a tourism-only website.
Balance four pillars equally:

• Nature Conservation
• Sustainable Tourism
• Environmental Education
• Institutional Transparency

--------------------------------------------------
VISUAL STYLE
--------------------------------------------------

• Cinematic and immersive.
• Full-width responsive layout.
• Large professional photography.
• Modern clean typography.
• Glassmorphism only for featured cards.
• Soft shadows and rounded corners.
• Elegant hover states.
• Minimal animations.
• Accessible WCAG 2.1 design.
• Mobile-first approach.

Avoid excessive futuristic effects.
